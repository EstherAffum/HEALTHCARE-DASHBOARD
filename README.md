# Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze patient demographics, medical conditions, hospital admissions, insurance coverage, medications, and billing information.

The dashboard transforms raw healthcare data into interactive visual insights that can support hospital management in understanding patient trends, monitoring financial performance, and making data-driven resource allocation decisions.

The dataset used for this project was obtained from **Kaggle**.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project was developed to:

* Analyze patient demographics by age, gender, and blood type.
* Identify trends in medical conditions and their occurrence over time.
* Examine insurance provider distribution and patient coverage.
* Analyze hospital billing amounts and admitted days.
* Identify frequently administered medications.
* Provide insights that can support hospital resource planning and operational decisions.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* **Patient Demographics:** Male and female patients are represented in nearly equal proportions.
* **Age Distribution:** Middle-aged patients represent the largest patient group, with **12,506 patients**.
* **Insurance:** Medicare and Cigna account for a significant proportion of patient insurance coverage.
* **Medical Conditions:** Diabetes and cancer are among the frequently occurring medical conditions in the dataset.
* **Medication:** Lipitor, Ibuprofen, and Aspirin are among the most frequently administered medications.
* **Financial Analysis:** The dataset records approximately **$1.028 billion in total billing**, highlighting the financial scale of hospital operations.
* **Hospital Utilization:** Patients accumulated **624,636 admitted days**, providing an important measure of hospital resource utilization.
* **Blood Types:** AB+ and O- are among the blood types with the highest representation in the dataset.

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the dashboard highlights several areas for potential improvement:

1. **Resource Allocation**
   Monitor demand for conditions such as diabetes and cancer to support appropriate staffing, equipment, and medical supply planning.

2. **Insurance Coverage**
   Explore partnerships and patient education initiatives that could improve insurance coverage and reduce out-of-pocket financial pressure.

3. **Medication Management**
   Use prescription and administration trends to support inventory planning and reduce the risk of medication shortages.

4. **Preventive Healthcare**
   Develop targeted awareness and preventive healthcare programs for conditions associated with lifestyle and chronic disease risks.

5. **Predictive Analytics**
   Extend the analysis with predictive models to forecast patient admissions, identify demand patterns, and support hospital capacity planning.
